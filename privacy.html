Section 01
Who We Are

Oceanix is a creator support platform that allows fans to send monetary treats to their favourite content creators in India. Oceanix is operated as an independent project and can be reached at oceanix.vercel.app.

Simple version: Oceanix is a platform where fans tip creators. We're a small independent project, not a large corporation. We treat your data with care.
Section 02
Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to run the platform. Here is exactly what we collect:

  • Google Account Info — your name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in via Google.
  • Creator Profile Info — if you become a creator: your display name, username, category, bio, UPI ID, and Razorpay/UPI payment link.
  • Treat Records — when you send a treat: the treat type, amount, message, your user ID, and the creator's user ID.
  • Messages — messages you send through the in-app chat.

We do not collect your payment card details, CVV, bank account information, or any sensitive financial data. All payment processing is handled by Razorpay.

Section 03
How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect strictly to operate Oceanix:

  • To create and manage your account on the platform.
  • To display creator profiles on the Browse page.
  • To process and record treat transactions.
  • To show you your treat history and fan stats.
  • To enable in-app messaging between fans and creators.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your personal information with anyone except as required by law or described in Section 4 (Payments).

Section 04
Payments & Razorpay

All payments on Oceanix are processed through Razorpay or directly via UPI payment links provided by creators. When you click to send a treat, you are redirected to a third-party payment page.

Important: Oceanix does not process payments directly. We do not store your card details, UPI PIN, or any payment credentials. All financial transactions happen on Razorpay's platform, which is governed by Razorpay's own Privacy Policy.

Creators are responsible for providing accurate payment links. Oceanix is not liable for failed or disputed payments. If you have a payment issue, please contact the creator directly or reach out to Razorpay support.

Section 05
Data Storage & Firebase

Oceanix uses Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication) to store user data and manage authentication. Your data is stored on Google's servers, protected by Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure.

  • Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth using Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0).
  • User data, creator profiles, treat records, and messages are stored in Cloud Firestore.
  • Data is stored in Google's data centres and subject to Google's data protection practices.

Firebase is GDPR-compliant and follows industry-standard security practices. For more information, see the Firebase Privacy Policy.

Section 06
Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data on Oceanix:

  • Access — You can view your profile, treat history, and creator page at any time within the app.
  • Edit — You can update your creator profile (name, bio, category, payment link) at any time from the Profile page.
  • Delete — You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 7 business days.
  • Portability — You can request a copy of your data by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address provided in Section 10.

Section 07
Cookies

Oceanix does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Firebase Authentication may use session storage to keep you signed in across browser sessions.

We do not use any third-party analytics services or advertising networks that place cookies on your device.

Section 08
Children's Privacy

Oceanix is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete that information.

Section 09
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Oceanix grows and new features are added. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Continued use of Oceanix after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
